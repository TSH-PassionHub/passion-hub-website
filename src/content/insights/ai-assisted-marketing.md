AI tools can draft copy, generate visuals, and suggest targeting in seconds. That speed is genuinely useful — but speed isn't the same as quality, and it's definitely not the same as a brand voice your audience recognizes.

## Where AI helps

We use AI as a support tool, not a shortcut, across a few specific stages:

- **Drafting first passes** of captions or ad copy, which our team then edits for tone and accuracy
- **Surfacing patterns** in performance data faster than manual review would
- **Generating visual concepts** to explore directions before final design work begins

## Where it doesn't

AI output tends to drift toward generic phrasing and safe, average choices — the opposite of what makes a brand memorable. Every AI-assisted output we use is reviewed, edited, and approved by an actual strategist before it reaches your audience. That review step is non-negotiable, because a brand voice is built over years and can be flattened in a single off-tone post.

The goal isn't to remove people from the process. It's to give our team more time to focus on the strategic thinking AI can't do — knowing your audience, your market, and what will actually move them.
